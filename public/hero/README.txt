Hero media for the Ironline home page.

  gym-poster.jpg   1920x1080 poster image. Shows first and is the fallback.
  gym.webm         12-second, 1920x1080 VP9 lifting clip. Loops silently.

The source clip is in /public/video/bg2.webm. Replace gym.webm and its poster with your own licensed footage if desired.
The video plays on small screens too. For reduced-motion or data-saver preferences, the poster is shown instead.
